# Handoff: exact reconstruction of the GraphSAGE PPI GO labels

## Executive result

We can exactly reproduce all 121 GraphSAGE label columns on the 4,268 independently resolved Entrez Gene IDs after collapsing repeated tissue instances. The compared matrix has 516,428 binary cells and contains zero false positives and zero false negatives under two independent implementations.

This equality applies to every node row whose Entrez identity was independently recovered. It does not itself identify the remaining unresolved graph nodes.

## Exact transformation

### 1. Recover Entrez Gene identities independently

Match each anonymous GraphSAGE tissue graph to its OhmNet tissue network using graph structure. Use Weisfeiler–Lehman refinement and feature-pattern constraints to recover the anonymous node-to-Entrez mapping without using labels. Collapse repeated tissue instances to one row per resolved Entrez Gene ID. This gives 4,268 resolved genes.

### 2. Build an ambiguity-preserving Entrez–UniProt map

Treat `gp2protein.geneid` as a bipartite graph, not a lookup dictionary.

- Keep legitimate one-to-many and many-to-one relationships.
- Do not arbitrarily choose one accession or one GeneID.
- Resolve mapping components before filtering to the GraphSAGE gene universe; otherwise an off-universe GeneID can be hidden and its accession misassigned.
- Use a unique primary-symbol fallback only when an accession lacks a historical GeneID edge and the symbol match is unambiguous.
- Resolve a square many-to-many component by symbols only when the symbols establish a unique bijection.
- Keep unresolved components expanded rather than forcing one-to-one mappings.

Examples retained as ambiguous include:

- P69905 → GeneIDs 3039 and 3040;
- P62158 → GeneIDs 801, 805, and 808;
- P62805 → multiple histone H4 GeneIDs;
- P0DMV8/P0DMV9 ↔ HSPA1A/HSPA1B, resolved only by a unique symbol bijection.

### 3. Correct the O95073/Q9Y620 component before graph restriction

The historical component contains:

- Q9Y620 ↔ GeneID 25788, symbol RAD54B;
- O95073 ↔ GeneID 100861412, symbol FSBP;
- an additional historical O95073 ↔ 25788 edge.

Do not project O95073/FSBP GO annotations to GeneID 25788/RAD54B. Retain:

```text
Q9Y620 → 25788
O95073 → 100861412
```

This decision is based on the complete mapping component and concordant symbols, not on label values. If the graph universe is applied first, GeneID 100861412 disappears and O95073 is incorrectly assigned to 25788, creating 13 false-positive label cells.

### 4. Filter GOA159 annotations

For every GAF row:

- exclude `NOT`;
- retain evidence codes `EXP`, `IDA`, `IEP`, `IGI`, `IMP`, and `ISS`;
- canonicalize alternate GO IDs;
- retain only the default relation for each aspect:
  - BP: `involved_in`
  - CC: `part_of`
  - MF: `enables`

Do not treat these qualified relations as ordinary binary membership:

- `colocalizes_with`
- `contributes_to`

This relation rule is essential. Including all positive relations created 901 excess gene-label assignments. Excluding `colocalizes_with` removed 501; excluding `contributes_to` removed 387. The remaining 13 were all caused by the O95073 mapping component.

### 5. Propagate through `is_a` only

For every retained direct annotation, assign the gene to the term and all of its transitive `is_a` ancestors. Do not propagate through `part_of`. Adding `part_of` worsens the match substantially.

### 6. Compare with GraphSAGE columns

The final transformed memberships exactly match all 121 columns on all 4,268 resolved genes.

- false positives: 0
- false negatives: 0
- exact columns: 121/121
- compared cells: 516,428

The 121 columns contain 118 distinct vectors. Three duplicated vector pairs each admit two exact GO IDs:

```text
columns 24 and 71: GO:0043228 or GO:0043232
columns 39 and 63: GO:0006464 or GO:0036211
columns 48 and 70: GO:0043230 or GO:1903561
```

The union of exact GO candidates is therefore 121 distinct terms: 85 BP, 26 CC, and 10 MF.

## Term-selection discovery

Do not calculate term prevalence only within the GraphSAGE genes. Calculate it in the full historical human annotation universe.

1. Use all GPI159-linked historical human GeneIDs.
2. Apply the exact evidence, relation, mapping, and `is_a` rules above.
3. Count distinct human GeneIDs per propagated GO term.

This produces 19,056 mapped human GeneIDs and 16,338 nonempty propagated terms.

The exact 121 GO candidates are:

- the top 121 terms by full-human prevalence; and
- equivalently, every term with at least 1,000 distinct human GeneIDs in release 159.

The boundary is 1,007 genes for rank 121 and 997 for rank 122. A fully independent standard-library/set implementation reproduces the 121/121 set equality.

Counting distinct Swiss-Prot/GPI accessions instead of GeneIDs produces the same 121-term set and the same ≥1,000 threshold result. Therefore the original code may have spoken in terms of proteins rather than genes.

Release 158 gives the same top-121 set but only 120 terms at a ≥1,000 threshold. Membership vectors strongly favor release 159.

## Selection-policy uniqueness check

A 24-configuration robustness grid varied evidence filters, relation handling, and propagation. Only the exact six-code/default-relation/`is_a` combination recovered the exact top-121 or ≥1,000 full-human term set. This provides a second line of evidence for the transformation beyond the exact GraphSAGE membership comparison.

## MSigDB result

Direct memberships in MSigDB 5.1, 5.2, and 6.0 do not reproduce any GraphSAGE label column exactly, even when every collection is searched.

- v5.1 closest mismatch: 307 genes;
- v5.2 closest mismatch: 10 genes;
- v6.0 closest mismatch: 10 genes;
- exact columns in each version: 0.

Thus, MSigDB may have provided term identities or metadata, but it did not directly provide the deposited memberships in the tested versions. MSigDB 5.0 is still untested.

## Important cautions

- Do not call the labels “121 Biological Process terms.” They comprise 85 BP, 26 CC, and 10 MF exact candidates.
- Do not collapse `contributes_to` or `colocalizes_with` into ordinary membership.
- Do not propagate `part_of`.
- Do not discard ambiguous mappings globally.
- Do not filter the mapping graph to GraphSAGE genes before resolving components.
- Do not state that there are unambiguously 118 source GO terms. There are 118 distinct vectors and 121 exact GO candidates; six duplicated columns are term-identity ambiguous from the matrix alone.
- Do not claim that MSigDB direct memberships reproduce the labels.
- The exact ontology date is still a robustness question, although the May-31 ontology already produces exact equality.
