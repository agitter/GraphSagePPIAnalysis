1. **Do not treat GAF `contributes_to` and `colocalizes_with` qualifier rows as ordinary unqualified gene-to-term membership.** Retaining only the default aspect relations (`involved_in`, `part_of`, and `enables`) removes 888 of the 901 discrepancies without losing a single deposited positive label.

## 1. What “residual” meant in the previous report

The previous model used:

- GOA human release 159;
- the accepted May-2016 UniProt↔GeneID projection;
- evidence codes `EXP, IDA, IEP, IGI, IMP, ISS`;
- every positive GAF relation, including `contributes_to` and `colocalizes_with`;
- `is_a` propagation with the June-1 ontology.

For each of the 4,268 resolved GeneIDs and each of the 121 GraphSAGE label columns, that model generated a binary prediction. A **residual gene-label difference** was one matrix cell where that prediction differed from the deposited label.

All 901 former residuals were false positives:

```text
reconstruction = 1
GraphSAGE deposited label = 0
```

There were no false negatives under that model. Thus “largest residual terms” meant **the GO label columns containing the greatest numbers of excess predicted genes**, not the largest GO terms in the ontology and not unmatched GO IDs.

For example, “catalytic activity” had 104 excess predicted genes; “protein complex” had 85; and the molecular-function root had 84. Each count is a count of GeneID × label-column disagreements.

### Interpretation

Depth and breadth **amplified** the former error: a qualified annotation made to a specific descendant can propagate to several broad ancestors. Broad roots and near-root terms therefore accumulated many excess genes.

Depth was not the root cause, however. Broad BP terms were reproduced exactly. The decisive distinction was that the legacy GAF qualifier relations involved here are aspect-specific:

- `colocalizes_with` occurs in Cellular Component;
- `contributes_to` occurs in Molecular Function;
- neither explains Biological Process membership.

Once those qualified relations are handled separately, the apparent association between shallow ontology depth and mismatch mostly disappears.

## 3. Uniform qualifier handling removes 888 of 901 differences

GAF 2.1 column 4 is a qualifier field. In release 159 the non-NOT positive qualifier values are `colocalizes_with` and `contributes_to`. These are not synonyms for the default aspect relations; they modify the interpretation of an annotation.

The release-159 GAF rows surviving the six-code evidence filter and mapping to the GraphSAGE universe included:

- 30,866 `involved_in` rows;
- 17,961 `part_of` rows;
- 10,218 `enables` rows;
- 578 `colocalizes_with` rows;
- 510 `contributes_to` rows.

The policy comparison is:

| Relation policy | FP | FN | Exact columns |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Include every positive relation | 901 | 0 | 89 |
| Exclude only `colocalizes_with` | 400 | 0 | 99 |
| Exclude only `contributes_to` | 514 | 0 | 98 |
| Retain only default relations | **13** | **0** | **108** |

The decomposition is exact:

- 501 former false positives depended on `colocalizes_with`;
- 387 depended on `contributes_to`;
- 13 remained under ordinary `involved_in` / `part_of` / `enables` annotations.

Of the original 901 differences:

- 20 were direct qualified annotations to the selected term;
- 868 arose when qualified annotations were propagated to ancestors;
- 3 remaining differences were direct default-relation annotations;
- 10 remaining differences were default-relation ancestor assignments.

Every deposited positive label remained supported after the qualifier-bearing rows were excluded. This is a global rule applied before label comparison, not per-term or per-gene pruning.

### Full historical mapping component

The earlier mapper built ambiguity components only after restricting GeneIDs to the GraphSAGE universe. That removed GeneID 100861412 from consideration and made the O95073 edge look like a usable mapping to 25788. Reconstructing the component **before** restricting to GraphSAGE exposes the conflict:

| Historical GeneID | Accession | GPI159 symbol | Independent GeneID symbol | In GraphSAGE |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| 25788 | Q9Y620 | RAD54B | RAD54B | yes |
| 25788 | O95073 | FSBP | RAD54B | yes |
| 25788 | A0A087X0H2 | not in GPI159 | RAD54B | yes |
| 100861412 | O95073 | FSBP | not needed for the GraphSAGE projection | no |

The correction is:

```text
retain Q9Y620 → GeneID 25788 (RAD54B)
retain O95073 → GeneID 100861412 (FSBP)
do not project O95073 → GeneID 25788
```

The mapping decision was made from the historical crosswalk component, GPI primary symbols, and the independent MSigDB-v5.2 Entrez symbol for 25788. The deposited labels were not used to choose the edge.

Current NCBI and UniProt records agree with these identities: GeneID 25788 is RAD54B, GeneID 100861412 is FSBP, Q9Y620 is RAD54B, and O95073 is FSBP. Current records do not prove that every 2016 cross-reference was identical, so the duplicate May-2016 edge remains a temporal provenance question. They do confirm that treating O95073/FSBP annotations as RAD54B annotations is not defensible without additional historical evidence.

## 7. Exact reconstruction and independent validation

The final transformation is:

1. Use GOA human release 159.
2. Map UniProt objects through the May-2016 historical crosswalk while preserving legitimate many-to-many components.
3. Resolve the O95073/Q9Y620 component using primary-symbol consistency before projecting to GraphSAGE GeneIDs.
4. Keep evidence codes `EXP, IDA, IEP, IGI, IMP, ISS`.
5. Exclude `NOT`, `contributes_to`, and `colocalizes_with` annotations from ordinary binary membership.
6. Propagate retained annotations through `is_a` only, including the directly annotated term.

## 8. Revised assessment of ontology drift

Before qualifier handling was tested, ontology drift seemed plausible because 878 of the 901 cells were ancestor-only assignments. That interpretation was incomplete.

After separating qualifier-bearing relations and resolving the O95073 component, the May-31 ontology closure already gives an exact matrix. Ontology drift is therefore **not required** to explain any deposited label.

The internal edge analyses also show why ontology drift was a poor explanation for the last 13 even before the mapping conflict was recognized:

- three were direct annotations and could not be removed by changing `is_a` edges;
- deleting immediate parent edges for the other ten would remove many correct genes along with the one excess gene;
- no tested edge subset improved the 13-cell result.

B105 is still worthwhile, but its role changes from “find the missing mechanism” to **robustness validation**:

- If the exact/nearest late-June ontology also gives zero differences, the relevant `is_a` closure was stable.
- If it changes the matrix, that would suggest the original generator used an earlier ontology snapshot, a precomputed annotation set, or another ontology serialization despite the release-159 GAF header.

## 10. What is now resolved and what remains open

### Resolved at transformation level

- Every deposited label column has an exact GO membership reconstruction.
- The complete 4,268 × 121 matrix is reproduced exactly.
- The relevant GOA release is strongly localized to release 159 rather than 158.
- GAF qualifier-bearing relations must not be treated as ordinary unqualified membership.
- The O95073/25788 historical cross-reference must not be used to project FSBP annotations onto RAD54B.
- Legitimate many-to-many mappings remain preserved; the correction is component-specific and symbol-supported.

### Still open

1. **Exact upstream source and code.** Exact equivalence does not prove whether the authors parsed GOA directly, used MSigDB term definitions with regenerated membership, used NCBI Gene, or consumed another processed product.
2. **Why these terms were selected.** There are 118 unique GO IDs represented by 121 columns, but the selection criterion is still not explained by a simple ≥15-gene threshold.
3. **Historical origin of the O95073→25788 duplicate edge.** A date-matched 2016 UniProt flat file, NCBI `gene_info`/`gene2accession`, or Bioconductor package should be used to determine whether it was a stale, erroneous, or intentionally broad cross-reference.
4. **Exact ontology robustness.** The June-29/July-1 ontology test remains valuable, even though ontology drift is no longer necessary.
5. **Mechanism behind the six-code evidence set.** Exhaustive testing identifies the set, but the originating pipeline rule or source-specific evidence policy remains to be located.
